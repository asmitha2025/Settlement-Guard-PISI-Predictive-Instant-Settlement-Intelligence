Problem Breakdown

Two Fundamentally Different Payment Failures

Most systems confuse authorization failure with post-capture settlement risk.

1. AUTHORIZATION FAILURE
• The payment attempt itself fails at checkout.
• Customer's issuing bank cannot approve the debit.
• Razorpay docs call this "beyond our control" and point to multi-terminal routing.
Handled by Smart Routing (Bygari et al., 2021).
ALREADY SOLVED BY RAZORPAY.
2. SETTLEMENT RISK
• The payment already succeeded and was captured.
• Funds are trapped onward in degraded partner bank CBS.
• Not a customer error — a severe merchant cash-flow crisis (T+2 to T+7 delay).
• Today, merchants must notice delays manually and pay 0.30%–0.50% fee.
NOTHING PREDICTS THIS TODAY. PISI BUILDS IT.
